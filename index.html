About

Hi, I’m Wang Zijun, an undergraduate in Communication Engineering at Northeastern University at Qinhuangdao. I am drawn to one question: how can we make systems more intelligent and dependable?

My interests span three connected areas: large language models and multi-agent systems, knowledge graphs and reliable AI, and communication and coding theory. I have contributed to work on multi-agent governance for bureaucratic-bias detection and Rule–LLM cascade routing for knowledge-graph entity resolution. At the IHUB Research Program, I also studied fault tolerance and computational efficiency in EVENODD coding.

Beyond research, I have worked on FPC/PCB hardware engineering and embedded development in industry. I value research that is theoretically sound and grounded in real systems. This site is where I collect ideas, document what I build, and connect with others.

Projects

Systems I’m helping build.

Three research efforts spanning system design and practical implementation.

MARS-Gov

Open-set standpoint screening and multi-agent governance for bureaucratic-bias detection in government text

Research collaboration · Co-author

A closed-loop multi-agent governance workflow: legal and policy evidence retrieval, open-set target screening, a dual-jury mechanism, risk routing, and verdict rewriting for verification.

  • Introduces a dual-jury mechanism with a fixed and dynamic “tenth juror” to identify affected groups beyond nine predefined bias categories.
  • Builds a normative-evidence context from Dutch anti-discrimination law, government accessibility guidance, and DGDB category definitions.
  • Uses risk-routing gates and verdict-rewriting verification to create an explainable, auditable governance loop.
Multi-AgentRAGOpen-Set ScreeningLLMPrompt Engineering

Rule–LLM Cascade Routing

Learning rule–LLM cascade routing for knowledge-graph entity resolution

Research collaboration · Co-author

Learns routing policies between rule systems and large language models to balance entity-resolution accuracy, inference cost, and processing efficiency.

  • Organizes accurate but costlier LLM decisions and efficient rule-based methods into a cooperative cascade.
  • Learns sample-level routing: easy cases stay with rules while difficult cases are sent to an LLM.
  • Evaluates the trade-off among accuracy, efficiency, and cost for knowledge-graph entity resolution.
Knowledge GraphEntity ResolutionLLMRule-based SystemsRouting

IMEX-FND

An interaction-aware mixture-of-experts framework for multimodal fake-news detection

Research project

Explicitly decomposes cross-modal interactions into five experts, using sample-level dynamic weights for fine-grained and interpretable decisions.

  • Builds a unified multimodal feature pipeline and an instance-level expert router.
  • Separates interactions into text uniqueness, image uniqueness, fusion uniqueness, synergy, and redundancy experts.
